# Perpetuo
Open-source multi-agent platform for structured citizen deliberation on the societal readiness of emerging technologies

Perpetuo combines scenario building, backcasting and visioning with a 
systemic assessment framework, made accessible to non-experts through 
AI agent facilitation. It is designed as reusable democratic 
infrastructure for technology governance, deployable by any European 
community.

---

## Why Perpetuo

Emerging technologies — from AI systems to advanced materials — are 
reshaping society faster than governance frameworks can adapt. Existing 
civic technology platforms excel at discussion structuring and opinion 
aggregation, but none provide citizens with structured methods to assess 
*where and how* to intervene in complex technological systems for 
maximum societal benefit.

Perpetuo addresses this gap by integrating two validated frameworks:
the European Commission's Foresight Menu and Donella Meadows' 
12 leverage points — operationalised together for the first time in an 
open-source, AI-facilitated citizen engagement context.

---

## The Four Agents

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| **Facilitator** | Guides citizens through foresight exercises (horizon scanning, scenario building, backcasting, visioning) |
| **Evidence Synthesiser** | Retrieves and maps information to Meadows' 12 leverage points |
| **Accessibility Mediator** | Adapts complexity, modality and framing to participant cognitive profile |
| **Bias Monitor** | Audits deliberation quality across discursive and perceptual dimensions |

All agent actions are provenance-tracked using W3C PROV. Every output 
carries full metadata: model version, prompt context, and decision 
rationale.

---

## Deliverables

1. **SRL Protocol** — open Societal Readiness Level specification in 
JSON-LD (CC0), structured around Meadows' 12 leverage points
2. **Agent Framework** — modular multi-agent system in Python/TypeScript 
(AGPL-3.0)
3. **Citizen Interface** — progressive web application (WCAG 2.1 AA)
4. **Deliberation Dataset Schema** — open schema for deliberation data 
(CC-BY-SA 4.0), enabling future research on cognitive diversity and 
technology governance
